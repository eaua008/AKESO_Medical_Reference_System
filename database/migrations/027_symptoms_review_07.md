## Palpitations  (`sym_palpitations`)

**Added to the description:** Most are caused by harmless extra beats (premature atrial or ventricular contractions); others include supraventricular tachycardia, atrial fibrillation or flutter, ventricular tachycardia and non-cardiac triggers such as thyrotoxicosis, anemia, caffeine and low potassium. An ECG recorded during symptoms is the key test.

**New causes:**
- Harmless extra beats (premature contractions)
- Supraventricular tachycardia
- Ventricular tachycardia
- Low potassium

**New red flags:**
- Heart rate over 120 or under 45 at rest
- Palpitations during exercise, or a family history of sudden death

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Palpitations: Thompson AD, Shea MJ. Palpitations. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Aug 2024. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/cardiovascular-disorders/symptoms-of-cardiovascular-disorders/palpitations

## Pink ring-shaped rash on the trunk (erythema marginatum)  (`sym_erythema_marginatum`)

**Added to the description:** The rash is flat or slightly raised, painless and leaves no scar, sometimes lasting less than a day, and is seen in fewer than 6% of children with acute rheumatic fever. It is one of the five major Jones criteria: diagnosis needs two major, or one major and two minor criteria, plus evidence of recent group A streptococcal infection.

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Rheumatic Fever: Weinberg GA. Rheumatic Fever. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Feb 2024. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/miscellaneous-bacterial-infections-in-infants-and-children/rheumatic-fever

## Pleuritic chest pain  (`sym_pleuritic_chest_pain`)

**Added to the description:** Pulmonary embolism comes with breathlessness, fast heart rate and sometimes coughing blood; pneumothorax with reduced breath sounds on one side; pericarditis with pain worse lying flat and better leaning forward and a friction rub. Pain that worsens with breathing or pressing on the chest does not by itself prove a chest-wall cause.

**New red flags:**
- Pain worse lying flat and better leaning forward (possible pericarditis)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Chest Pain: Thompson AD, Shea MJ. Chest Pain. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Aug 2024. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/cardiovascular-disorders/symptoms-of-cardiovascular-disorders/chest-pain

## Productive cough (sputum)  (`sym_productive_cough`)

**Added to the description:** Chronic bronchitis is defined as a productive cough for 3 months a year for 2 years in a row, usually in smokers. The color or thickness of sputum does not reliably tell bacterial from viral infection.

**New causes:**
- Heart failure (pink frothy sputum)

**New red flags:**
- Known HIV infection or contact with someone with TB

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Cough in Adults: Dezube R. Cough in Adults. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Nov 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/pulmonary-disorders/symptoms-of-pulmonary-disorders/cough-in-adults

## Profuse watery ("rice-water") diarrhea  (`sym_watery_diarrhea`)

**Added to the description:** Cholera diarrhea starts suddenly and is painless, often with vomiting, and the white stool contains no fecal material. Without treatment it causes low blood potassium, acidosis and circulatory collapse with a death rate above 50%, falling to about 1% with prompt rehydration.

**New red flags:**
- Several cases in a household or community, especially after flooding or water contamination

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Cholera: Bush LM, Vazquez-Pertejo MT. Cholera. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Jun 2024. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/infectious-diseases/gram-negative-bacilli/cholera

## Puffy eyelids and face (periorbital edema)  (`sym_periorbital_edema`)

**Added to the description:** Post-infectious glomerulonephritis typically begins 6 to 21 days after a throat or skin infection, with edema, blood and protein in the urine, reduced urine output and high blood pressure; complement C3 is low and usually returns to normal within 6 to 8 weeks. Angioedema causes sudden, uneven facial or lip swelling that can involve the airway.

**New red flags:**
- Swelling 1 to 3 weeks after a sore throat or skin infection, with dark urine

**New references:**

## Purple rash that does not fade when pressed (non-blanching rash)  (`sym_nonblanching_rash`)

**Added to the description:** In meningococcal disease the rash appears soon after onset, mainly on the trunk and legs; in fulminant cases the spots merge into bruises and dark patches of dead skin, with shock and DIC. Antibiotics (a third-generation cephalosporin) are started immediately, before tests come back.

**New red flags:**
- Spots merging into large bruises or dark patches

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Meningococcal Diseases: Bush LM, Vazquez-Pertejo MT. Meningococcal Diseases. Merck Manual Professional Version. ## Red eyes (conjunctivitis / conjunctival suffusion)  (`sym_conjunctivitis`)

**Added to the description:** Most red eyes are infectious or allergic conjunctivitis, which causes no real pain, no light sensitivity and no corneal staining; viral and bacterial conjunctivitis cannot be told apart reliably on clinical grounds.

**New red flags:**
- Shingles rash on the forehead or nose tip with a red eye

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Red Eye: Brady CJ. Red Eye. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Oct 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/eye-disorders/symptoms-of-ophthalmologic-disorders/red-eye

## Retro-orbital pain (pain behind the eyes)  (`sym_retroorbital_pain`)

**Added to the description:** In dengue it starts abruptly with fever, headache, lower back pain and prostration after a 3 to 15 day incubation; warning signs appear around days 4 to 7. Deep aching or throbbing eye pain in someone without fever suggests serious eye disease such as glaucoma, uveitis, scleritis or orbital cellulitis.

**New causes:**
- Glaucoma
- Scleritis or uveitis
- Chikungunya

**New red flags:**
- Halos around lights with vomiting (possible acute glaucoma)

**New references:**

## Right lower abdominal pain (right lower quadrant)  (`sym_rlq_pain`)

**Added to the description:** Classic signs include tenderness at McBurney point with rebound, and the Rovsing, psoas and obturator signs, but more than half of patients present atypically, especially older adults and pregnant women. Classic cases are diagnosed clinically, while CT or ultrasound helps when the picture is unclear.

**New causes:**
- Typhoid fever (inflamed or perforated ileum)

**New red flags:**
- Abdominal pain in a pregnant woman or older adult (appendicitis is often atypical)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Appendicitis: Ansari P. Appendicitis. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Jul 2024. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/gastrointestinal-disorders/acute-abdomen-and-surgical-gastroenterology/appendicitis

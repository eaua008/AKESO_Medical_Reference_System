## Spoon-shaped, brittle nails (koilonychia)  (`sym_koilonychia`)

**Added to the description:** It appears in severe iron deficiency together with pica, a smooth sore tongue and cracks at the mouth corners. Iron deficiency is mostly due to blood loss, so in men and women past menopause the bowel is checked for hidden bleeding, which can be the only sign of a gut cancer.

**New red flags:**
- Iron deficiency in a man or a woman past menopause (look for bowel bleeding)

**New references:**

## Stiff neck (neck stiffness)  (`sym_neck_stiffness`)

**Added to the description:** Fever and neck stiffness may be missing in newborns, infants, older adults, people with weak immunity and heavy drinkers, so a lumbar puncture should be considered early. Bacterial meningitis is an emergency: antibiotics (with steroids) are given within 30 minutes, and brain imaging comes before the lumbar puncture only if there is papilledema or a focal deficit.

**New red flags:**
- Fever with drowsiness in an infant or older adult, even without a stiff neck

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Acute Bacterial Meningitis: Klein RS. Acute Bacterial Meningitis. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Sep 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/neurologic-disorders/meningitis/acute-bacterial-meningitis

## Sudden hot, red, swollen big-toe joint (podagra)  (`sym_podagra`)

**Added to the description:** Common triggers are injury, surgery, infection, thiazide diuretics, starting urate-lowering drugs, purine-rich food and alcohol. Diagnosis is by finding needle-shaped, negatively birefringent urate crystals in joint fluid; the blood urate level is normal in at least 30% of people during a flare.

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Gout: Keller SF. Gout. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Jul 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/musculoskeletal-and-connective-tissue-disorders/crystal-induced-arthritides/gout

## Suprapubic pain  (`sym_suprapubic_pain`)

**Added to the description:** In cystitis it comes on suddenly with frequency, urgency and burning while passing small volumes. A positive nitrite test is highly specific for bacteria, and leukocyte esterase is fairly sensitive; dysuria with white cells but no bacteria suggests urethritis or an STI.

**New causes:**
- Prostatitis

**New red flags:**
- Repeated infections, or symptoms in a man

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Bacterial Urinary Tract Infections: Imam TH. Bacterial Urinary Tract Infections. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Mar 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/infectious-diseases/urinary-tract-infections/bacterial-urinary-tract-infections

## Sweating (diaphoresis)  (`sym_diaphoresis`)

**Added to the description:** Generalised sweating can come from lymphoma or leukemia, tuberculosis, endocarditis, hyperthyroidism, low blood sugar, drugs, opioid withdrawal and menopause. Sweating that is mainly at night points to cancer or infection, and sweating on only one side suggests a nerve problem.

**New causes:**
- Tuberculosis
- Lymphoma or leukemia
- Endocarditis
- Opioid or alcohol withdrawal

**New red flags:**
- Sweating on only one side of the body

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Hyperhidrosis: Das S. Hyperhidrosis. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Jun 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/dermatologic-disorders/sweating-disorders/hyperhidrosis

**New disease links:**

## Swelling at the front of the neck (goiter)  (`sym_goiter`)

**Added to the description:** Iodine deficiency is the leading cause worldwide. Most goiters cause no symptoms, but large ones can cause swallowing difficulty, hoarseness or a feeling of fullness in the throat; assessment uses thyroid function tests, ultrasound, thyroid antibodies and sometimes an iodine uptake scan.

**New causes:**
- Goitrogens in food (cassava, cruciferous vegetables)
- Amiodarone or lithium

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Simple Nontoxic Goiter: Boucai L. Simple Nontoxic Goiter. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Jan 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/endocrine-and-metabolic-disorders/thyroid-disorders/simple-nontoxic-goiter

## Swollen lymph nodes (lymphadenopathy)  (`sym_lymphadenopathy`)

**Added to the description:** Nodes in one area for less than 2 weeks are usually from a local infection, while nodes in several areas suggest a systemic illness such as mononucleosis, lymphoma or lupus. Without red flags, nodes can be watched for up to 4 weeks; steroids should not be given empirically because they can mask lymphoma or leukemia.

**New red flags:**
- A node larger than 2 cm, or an enlarged spleen

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Lymphadenopathy: Douketis JD. Lymphadenopathy. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Mar 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/cardiovascular-disorders/lymphatic-disorders/lymphadenopathy

**New disease links:**
- tuberculosis (3/10, x0.4)

## Thin wavy lines between fingers or on wrists (burrows)  (`sym_burrows`)

**Added to the description:** Itching usually starts 4 to 6 weeks after a first infestation (sooner on re-infestation), and the face is spared in adults. Scrapings from a burrow can show the mite under a microscope, though a negative result does not rule scabies out; dermoscopy shows a 'delta-wing jet' sign.

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Scabies: Dinulos JGH. Scabies. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Nov 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/dermatologic-disorders/parasitic-skin-infections/scabies

## Unintentional weight loss  (`sym_weight_loss`)

**Added to the description:** Weight loss despite a good appetite suggests hyperthyroidism, uncontrolled diabetes or malabsorption, while weight loss with a poor appetite suggests depression, cancer or medicines. Baseline tests usually include a chest X-ray, urinalysis, blood count, ESR or CRP, HIV test, chemistry and TSH.

**New causes:**
- Medicines
- Substance use

**New red flags:**
- Bone pain or swollen lymph nodes

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Involuntary Weight Loss: Wasserman MR. Involuntary Weight Loss. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Feb 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/special-subjects/nonspecific-symptoms/involuntary-weight-loss

## Urinary frequency & urgency  (`sym_urinary_frequency`)

**Added to the description:** Frequency means passing normal or small volumes often, unlike polyuria, where the total volume is over 3 L a day. Getting up at night with a weak stream suggests prostate enlargement.

**New causes:**
- Kidney or bladder stones

**New red flags:**
- Leg weakness, numbness around the buttocks or loss of bladder control (possible spinal cord compression)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Urinary Frequency: Maddukuri G. Urinary Frequency. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Sep 2024. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/genitourinary-disorders/symptoms-of-genitourinary-disorders/urinary-frequency

## Visual aura  (`sym_visual_aura`)

**Added to the description:** About a quarter of people with migraine have aura, usually before the headache and sometimes without any headache. In older adults, aura without headache can be mistaken for a transient ischemic attack.

**New causes:**
- Arterial dissection or vasculitis (rare mimics)

**New red flags:**
- Aura with no headache in an older adult (consider TIA)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Migraine: Silberstein SD. Migraine. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Feb 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/neurologic-disorders/headache/migraine

## Vomiting after coughing fits (post-tussive vomiting)  (`sym_posttussive_vomiting`)

**Added to the description:** Pertussis runs over 6 to 10 weeks; infants under 6 months are at highest risk and may have choking or blue spells instead of a whoop. Pertussis should be suspected with a cough of 2 weeks or more plus a whoop, coughing fits or vomiting after coughing, and PCR is the preferred test.

**New references:**

## Vomiting blood (hematemesis)  (`sym_hematemesis`)

**Added to the description:** Coffee-ground vomit suggests the bleeding has slowed or stopped, whereas bright red blood suggests active bleeding. Initial care is airway protection, large IV lines, fluids and transfusion as needed, an IV proton pump inhibitor and endoscopy.

**New causes:**
- Bleeding in severe dengue
- Swallowed blood from a nosebleed

**New red flags:**
- Pale, sweaty skin

**New references:**

## Waking at night to urinate (nocturia)  (`sym_nocturia`)

**Added to the description:** Comparing how much is drunk with how much is passed (and a 24-hour urine collection if unclear) separates high urine output from frequent small voids. Troublesome nocturia often improves by drinking less in the evening.

## Wheezing  (`sym_wheezing`)

**Added to the description:** Medicines such as beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors and NSAIDs can cause it, and in children bronchiolitis and inhaled foreign bodies are important. A wheeze that stays in one place suggests a tumor or foreign body, and noise on breathing in suggests stridor rather than wheeze.

**New causes:**
- Medicines (beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors, NSAIDs)

**New red flags:**
- Drowsiness or exhaustion during an asthma attack

**New references:**

## White patches in the mouth (oral thrush)  (`sym_oral_thrush`)

**Added to the description:** The diagnosis is usually clinical, and a KOH preparation of a scraping shows yeast and pseudohyphae. Treatment options include clotrimazole troches, nystatin suspension or oral fluconazole.

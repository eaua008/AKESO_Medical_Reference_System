## Right upper abdominal pain (right upper quadrant)  (`sym_ruq_pain`)

**Added to the description:** Acute cholecystitis feels like biliary colic but is more severe and lasts over 6 hours, with a positive Murphy sign and guarding; ultrasound is the best first test. In dengue, abdominal pain and an enlarging liver are warning signs of severe disease.

**New causes:**
- Right kidney stone or kidney infection
- Dengue (enlarged, tender liver)

**New red flags:**
- Pain lasting more than 6 hours with fever (possible acute cholecystitis)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Acute Cholecystitis: Saiman Y. Acute Cholecystitis. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Oct 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/hepatic-and-biliary-disorders/gallbladder-and-bile-duct-disorders/acute-cholecystitis

## Runny nose (coryza)  (`sym_rhinorrhea`)

**Added to the description:** Rhinoviruses cause about half of colds; symptoms start 1 to 3 days after exposure and usually clear within about 10 days. Thick, colored mucus does not by itself mean a bacterial infection.

**New red flags:**
- Symptoms lasting more than 10 days, or worsening after first improving (possible bacterial sinusitis)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Common Cold: Katz S. Common Cold. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Jul 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/infectious-diseases/respiratory-viruses/common-cold

## Seizures (fits or convulsions)  (`sym_seizures`)

**Added to the description:** Status epilepticus is a convulsion lasting more than 5 minutes or repeated seizures without recovery in between, and needs an IV benzodiazepine promptly. Tongue biting, incontinence and prolonged confusion afterwards favour a seizure over a faint.

**New causes:**
- Brain tumor
- Drug toxicity
- Cerebral malaria

**New red flags:**
- Seizure in someone with cancer or a weakened immune system

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Seizure Disorders: Adamolekun B. Seizure Disorders. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Mar 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/neurologic-disorders/seizure-disorders/seizure-disorders

## Sensitivity to light (photophobia)  (`sym_photophobia`)

**Added to the description:** In true photophobia from eye disease, light shone into the healthy eye causes pain in the affected eye, as with corneal injury or uveitis. In migraine it comes with nausea and sensitivity to sound and smells, and patients prefer a dark, quiet room.

**New red flags:**
- Halos around lights or vomiting with eye pain (possible acute glaucoma)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Eye Pain: Brady CJ. Eye Pain. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Oct 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/eye-disorders/symptoms-of-ophthalmologic-disorders/eye-pain

**New disease links:**

## Shaky hands (tremor)  (`sym_tremor`)

**Added to the description:** A tremor while reaching for a target (intention tremor) suggests cerebellar disease. Hyperthyroidism and drugs such as lithium, SSRIs, valproate and theophylline cause or worsen tremor.

**New causes:**
- Cerebellar disease (tremor on reaching for a target)
- SSRIs and theophylline

**New red flags:**
- Tremor starting before age 50 with no family history

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Tremor: Rajput A, Noyes E. Tremor. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Mar 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/neurologic-disorders/movement-and-cerebellar-disorders/tremor

## Shortness of breath (dyspnea)  (`sym_dyspnea`)

**Added to the description:** Wheeze points to asthma or COPD, stridor to upper airway obstruction, and crackles to heart failure, pneumonia or interstitial lung disease. A normal oxygen saturation does not exclude pulmonary embolism.

**New causes:**
- Pneumothorax
- Interstitial lung disease
- Physical deconditioning

**New red flags:**
- Using the neck and shoulder muscles to breathe

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Dyspnea: Dezube R. Dyspnea. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Nov 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/pulmonary-disorders/symptoms-of-pulmonary-disorders/dyspnea

## Signs of dehydration (sunken eyes, dry mouth, intense thirst)  (`sym_dehydration`)

**Added to the description:** Mild volume loss (under 5%) gives thirst and reduced urine; moderate loss (5 to 10%) adds dizziness on standing; severe loss (over 10%) leads to shock with low blood pressure, confusion and slow capillary refill. Skin turgor and a dry mouth are unreliable signs in older adults.

**New causes:**
- Burns or bleeding

**New red flags:**
- Infant or older adult with ongoing diarrhea or vomiting

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Volume Depletion: Lewis JL III. Volume Depletion. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Jun 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/endocrine-and-metabolic-disorders/fluid-metabolism/volume-depletion

**New disease links:**

- type2_diabetes (2/10, x0.3)

## Skin rash  (`sym_rash`)

**Added to the description:** Lesions are described by their size (under or over 10 mm) and type, and their arrangement gives clues: target lesions suggest erythema multiforme, grouped vesicles herpes simplex, and a band on one side zoster. Redness can be subtle on darker skin.

**New causes:**
- Erythema multiforme

**New red flags:**
- A black, scab-like sore (eschar)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Description of Skin Lesions: Benedetti J. Description of Skin Lesions. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Aug 2026. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/dermatologic-disorders/approach-to-the-dermatologic-patient/description-of-skin-lesions

## Slurred or difficult speech  (`sym_speech_difficulty`)

**Added to the description:** In Broca (expressive) aphasia speech is halting but understanding is preserved; in Wernicke (receptive) aphasia speech flows but makes little sense and understanding is poor. Assessment covers spontaneous speech, naming, repetition and comprehension, followed by brain imaging.

**New causes:**
- Encephalitis
- Brain tumor or dementia (gradual onset)

**New red flags:**
- Speech that gets steadily worse over weeks

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Aphasia: Huang J. Aphasia. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Sep 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/neurologic-disorders/function-and-dysfunction-of-the-cerebral-lobes/aphasia

## Sore throat  (`sym_sore_throat`)

**Added to the description:** Group A streptococcus causes about 10 to 25% of cases in adults. Testing is reasonable when two or more Centor criteria are present (tonsil exudate, tender neck nodes, fever, no cough), and severe pain with a normal-looking throat suggests epiglottitis.

**New causes:**
- Epiglottitis
- Peritonsillar abscess

**New red flags:**
- Severe pain but the throat looks normal (possible epiglottitis)

**New references:**
- Merck Manual Professional - Sore Throat: Fried MP. Sore Throat. Merck Manual Professional Version. Rahway (NJ): Merck & Co.; reviewed Mar 2025. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/approach-to-the-patient-with-nasal-and-pharyngeal-symptoms/sore-throat
